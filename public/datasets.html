Dataset information

Reviewed speech samples with open licensing.

Magnexis Voice aims to make small CC0 speech-data samples useful for experimentation and research while keeping pending, rejected and flagged recordings out of exports.

Scripted speech

Volunteers read supplied sentences aloud. Independent approvals allow text and voice recordings to be paired for speech recognition testing.

Spontaneous speech

Volunteers answer open-ended prompts. Their reviewed clips may include separately validated human transcriptions.

What a ZIP sample contains

Archives contain up to ten approved clips, CSV metadata and CC0 dedication information. Exports exclude users' email addresses and display names. The pilot does not have a verified large publicly released corpus; download availability depends on accepted real contributions.

CC0 and privacy

CC0 dedicated material may be widely reused, including commercially. Once distributed, copies may not be recallable. Record only your own voice; do not include identifying details.
