About the project

Community voices for open speech technology.

Magnexis Voice is an independently built project exploring community-collected speech data. Its purpose is to let volunteers submit their own recordings, review clip quality and build small reusable samples for research.

Why speech data?

Speech recognition needs to handle many accents, speaking styles, speeds and microphones. Diverse, reviewed data can help researchers study real-world voice interfaces and accessibility.

How we review

Contributors can validate other people's recordings, review original sentence prompts, and independently check spontaneous-speech transcriptions. Duplicate or self-votes are prevented.

Consent and privacy

Contributors must understand the CC0 dedication and avoid recording other people or disclosing personal information. Distributed public-domain recordings may be impossible to recall.

Status: Version 1.25.0 is a locally tested functional pilot, not a verified production-audited public collection service. This is an independent project, not affiliated with Mozilla Common Voice.
