Contribution workflow

How a recording becomes a validated speech clip.

Magnexis Voice gives volunteers simple, separate ways to record speech, listen to clips and review the words they contain.

1. Speak

Record prompted sentences in batches of five or respond to a spontaneous-speech prompt. Preview, retry or skip recordings.

2. Listen

Independent community listeners assess clip quality. Two different positive reviewers are required before acceptance.

3. Write

Propose original English sentences and help review whether they suit voice collection.

4. Transcribe

Listen to approved spontaneous clips, write transcripts and have those transcripts reviewed by separate contributors.

Tips for useful contributions

Speak clearly and naturally in a quiet room. Check background noise. Contribute only your own voice, avoid names, addresses and other sensitive details, and review the CC0 consent notice before submitting.
